# Process overview

## What I built

Ive built a planet orbiting game where you must avoid obstacles for as long as possible by changing your direction of orbit around the planet.

## The moments that mattered

Enemies were too hard to avoid. claude had a reasonable thought to keep the player colour bright and the enemey colour red, so that it would stand out and feel really speaerate. While play testeing I found that that wasnt enough, the obstacles all being the same colour, having some move and some not, all with a restricted lifespan but no way of knowing what will doe first unless you tracked 5 things independantly all at once. I decided to expand the colour pallete and get claude to make it so that each individual onstacle changed colour in line with its remaining lifespan, and so that when it was activley dying it coudnt kill you back. This immideitley made the game actually possiblebe to play for more than 15 seconds, and allowed me to get a top score over 400! Reaching a point where the main game mechanics are pretty good but wanting more from it, ive started giving claude very vague prompts and letting it run free to change whatever it wants to to hopefully get closer to my mystical aspirations. On one occasion asking claude "give the death screen more whimsy, change anything about it that you think will make it better" resulted in adding a changing roster of''you suck' quips, flashing rainbow score, moving 'try again' text and a colourful death explosion when you collide with an obstacle. while not things that I couldnt have envisioned myself they are definetly not all things that would have immediatley come to mind.
